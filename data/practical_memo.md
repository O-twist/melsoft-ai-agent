# Marking memo: PC assembly practical (Grade 10 CAT)

The rubric decides the mark. This memo tells markers what to look for in each attempt log.

## Build order (15)

- The expected order is: motherboard, CPU, RAM, GPU, SSD, PSU.
- The motherboard goes in first because everything else sits on it. Power goes in last.
- Each part tried out of turn costs marks, even if the learner corrected it straight away. Code sets a ceiling from the log, so a mark above it is sent back.

## Correct installation (15)

- Full marks need all six parts in the right slots and a finished build.
- A part dropped outside its slot and then corrected is a minor slip. A build left unfinished is in the middle band at best.

## Efficiency and independence (10)

- The target time is four minutes (240 seconds), with no hints.
- Each hint costs marks. Twice the target time or more caps this criterion at half marks.

## Key terms

motherboard, CPU, RAM, GPU, SSD, PSU, order, slot
