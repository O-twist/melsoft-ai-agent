Grade 10 History class test: answers

Question 1
1.1 The Bastille was stormed in 1798
1.2 The estates were the clergy and the nobility and the king
1.3 The slogan was long live the king
1.4 France was in debt after its wars, and the Third Estate paid unfair taxes while the clergy and nobles paid almost nothing
1.5 King Louis XVI was executed in 1793

Question 2
2.1 Ships left Europe with guns and cloth, sold them in Africa for enslaved people, and took them to the Americas where they bought sugar and cotton to sell back in Europe
2.2 The Middle Passage was the voyage across the Atlantic Ocean that carried enslaved Africans to the Americas
2.3 William Wilberforce campaigned in the British Parliament to end the trade
2.4 Africa lost millions of young people who could have worked the land, and the trade caused wars and raids between groups who captured people to sell

Question 3
3.1 The VOC arrived at the Cape in 1652
3.2 The party was led by Jan van Riebeeck
3.3 It was a refreshment station to give fresh food and water to ships sailing to the East
3.4 The Khoikhoi lost their grazing land and their cattle, and many died in the smallpox epidemic of 1713
3.5 Slavery was abolished at the Cape in 1838

Question 4
4.1 The ideas of the French Revolution spread in many ways. Napoleon's armies conquered much of Europe and brought new laws with them. The Declaration of the Rights of Man inspired people who had no rights. In Haiti, Toussaint L'Ouverture led a slave revolt and won freedom. Later, people in other countries demanded constitutions and democracy, and pamphlets and newspapers carried the ideas across borders.
