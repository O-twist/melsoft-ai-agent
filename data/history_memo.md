# Marking memo: Grade 10 History class test (40 marks)

The memo decides the mark for each part. Give the marks for an answer that matches the memo, even when the learner words it differently.

- Questions with several points: one mark per correct point (two where the memo says so), up to the marks for the part.
- Accept any accurate point the memo does not list, if it answers the question.
- Spelling does not cost marks if the meaning is clear.

## Question 1: The French Revolution (12)

- **1.1** (1) In which year was the Bastille stormed? Answer: 1789
- **1.2** (3) Name the three estates of pre-revolutionary France. Answer: The clergy, the nobility and the commoners (Third Estate).
- **1.3** (3) What was the slogan of the Revolution? Answer: Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
- **1.4** (4) Give two causes of the French Revolution. Answer: Any two: debt and bankruptcy, unfair taxes on the Third Estate, food shortages and the price of bread, Enlightenment ideas, the privileges of the first two estates.
- **1.5** (1) Which king was executed in 1793? Answer: Louis XVI

## Question 2: The transatlantic slave trade (10)

- **2.1** (3) Explain the triangular trade. Answer: Ships sailed from Europe with goods to Africa, carried enslaved Africans to the Americas, and took raw materials back to Europe.
- **2.2** (2) What was the Middle Passage? Answer: The voyage across the Atlantic that carried enslaved Africans to the Americas.
- **2.3** (1) Name one person who campaigned to abolish the slave trade. Answer: Any one: William Wilberforce, Olaudah Equiano, Thomas Clarkson.
- **2.4** (4) Give two effects of the slave trade on Africa. Answer: Any two: loss of young people and labour, wars and raids between groups, damage to local economies, families and communities torn apart.

## Question 3: The Cape under the VOC (10)

- **3.1** (1) In which year did the VOC set up a station at the Cape? Answer: 1652
- **3.2** (1) Who led the VOC party? Answer: Jan van Riebeeck
- **3.3** (2) Why did the VOC set up the station? Answer: To supply fresh food and water to its ships sailing to and from the East.
- **3.4** (4) Give two consequences of Dutch settlement for the Khoikhoi. Answer: Any two: loss of land, loss of cattle, smallpox and other diseases, being forced into labour as servants.
- **3.5** (2) In which year was slavery abolished at the Cape? Answer: 1834

## Question 4: Extended writing (8)

- **4.1** (8) Write a paragraph explaining how the ideas of the French Revolution spread beyond France. Answer: Any four, 2 marks each: Napoleon's wars and conquests; the ideas of rights and the Enlightenment; the Haitian slave revolt under Toussaint L'Ouverture; nationalism, constitutions and democracy in other countries; pamphlets, newspapers and print.

## Key terms

revolution, slave, trade, colony, cape, france, king, enlightenment
