# Marking memo: Grade 10 Physical Sciences class test (40 marks)

The memo decides the mark for each part. Give the marks for an answer that matches the memo, even when the learner words it differently.

- Method marks: when the final answer is wrong but the method is right, give about half the marks for that part.
- An error carried forward from an earlier part is penalised once only.
- A calculated answer without its unit loses one mark.

## Question 1: Matter and materials (8)

- **1.1** (2) Define an element. Answer: A pure substance made of only one type of atom.
- **1.2** (2) Classify salt water as an element, a compound or a mixture. Answer: A (homogeneous) mixture.
- **1.3** (2) Name the change of phase from liquid to gas. Answer: Evaporation (boiling).
- **1.4** (2) Is air a pure substance? Give a reason. Answer: No, air is a mixture of gases.

## Question 2: Atomic structure (10)

- **2.1** (2) Sodium has atomic number 11. How many protons does a sodium atom have? Answer: 11
- **2.2** (2) How many neutrons are in an atom of sodium-23? Answer: 12
- **2.3** (3) Write the sp-notation (electron configuration) for sodium. Answer: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹
- **2.4** (1) What is the charge on a proton? Answer: Positive (+1).
- **2.5** (2) Define isotopes. Answer: Atoms of the same element with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.

## Question 3: Chemical change (8)

- **3.1** (3) Balance the equation: H₂ + O₂ → H₂O Answer: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
- **3.2** (2) Calculate the molar mass of water (H = 1, O = 16). Answer: 18 g·mol⁻¹
- **3.3** (3) State the law of conservation of mass. Answer: In a closed system, mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

## Question 4: Motion (14)

- **4.1** (3) A car travels 100 m in 20 s. Calculate its average speed. Answer: 5 m·s⁻¹
- **4.2** (4) A car speeds up from rest to 20 m·s⁻¹ in 4 s. Calculate its acceleration. Answer: 5 m·s⁻²
- **4.3** (3) Explain the difference between distance and displacement. Answer: Distance is a scalar (total path length); displacement is a vector (change in position, with direction).
- **4.4** (2) Name the SI unit of force. Answer: The newton (N).
- **4.5** (2) Name an instrument used to measure time in a motion experiment. Answer: Ticker timer or stopwatch.

## Key terms

atom, element, mixture, proton, electron, mass, speed, acceleration
