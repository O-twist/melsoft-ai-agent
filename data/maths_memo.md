# Marking memo: Grade 10 Mathematics class test (40 marks)

The memo decides the mark for each part. Give the marks for an answer that matches the memo, even when the learner words it differently.

- Method marks: when the final answer is wrong but the method is right, give about half the marks for that part.
- An error carried forward from an earlier part is penalised once only.
- A decimal comma (0,6) and a decimal point (0.6) are both correct.

## Question 1: Algebraic expressions (10)

- **1.1** (3) Simplify (2x + 3)(x - 4). Answer: 2x² - 5x - 12
- **1.2** (2) Factorise x² - 9. Answer: (x - 3)(x + 3)
- **1.3** (3) Factorise 2x² + 7x + 3. Answer: (2x + 1)(x + 3)
- **1.4** (2) Simplify (2a³)². Answer: 4a⁶

## Question 2: Equations and inequalities (12)

- **2.1** (2) Solve 3x - 7 = 11. Answer: x = 6
- **2.2** (3) Solve x² - 5x + 6 = 0. Answer: x = 2 or x = 3
- **2.3** (2) Solve 2x + 1 > 7. Answer: x > 3
- **2.4** (5) Solve simultaneously: x + y = 7 and x - y = 1. Answer: x = 4 and y = 3

## Question 3: Functions and graphs (10)

- **3.1** (2) For f(x) = 2x - 4, find f(3). Answer: f(3) = 2
- **3.2** (2) Find the x-intercept of f. Answer: (2; 0)
- **3.3** (2) Find the y-intercept of f. Answer: (0; -4)
- **3.4** (2) Write down the gradient of f. Answer: 2
- **3.5** (2) For g(x) = x² - 1, find g(-2). Answer: 3

## Question 4: Trigonometry (8)

- **4.1** (2) In right-angled triangle ABC the hypotenuse is 10 cm and the side opposite θ is 6 cm. Find sin θ. Answer: sin θ = 6/10 = 0.6
- **4.2** (3) Find the length of the side adjacent to θ. Answer: 8 cm
- **4.3** (3) Find tan θ. Answer: tan θ = 6/8 = 0.75

## Key terms

simplify, factorise, solve, x, gradient, intercept, sin, tan
