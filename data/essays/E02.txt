Technology good or bad

Technology is good for young people in south africa. i think it has done more good then harm. we use phones every day and it help us alot.

First, you can learn on the internet. when i dont understand in class i watch a video and then i understand it better. also we can do research for projects fast.

Second you can talk to your family who is far. my uncle work in Cape Town and we whatsapp him every week.

But technology is also bad sometimes. some children are on there phones all night and they are tired at school. and there is cyber bullying which make people sad.

In conclusion technology is more good then bad because it help us learn and talk to people.
