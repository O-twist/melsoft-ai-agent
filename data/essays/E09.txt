A Generation Connected

It is easy to blame technology for everything that is wrong with teenagers. However, if we look carefully at the lives of young South Africans, technology has clearly done more good than harm.

To begin with, technology has made knowledge cheap. A second-hand smartphone and a library Wi-Fi connection give a learner access to lessons from the best teachers in the world. My older sister taught herself coding from free online courses and now works as a junior developer. She is the first person in our family with a salary.

Furthermore, technology gives young people a voice. Movements started by students have used social media to make leaders listen. Young poets, musicians and comedians can find an audience without waiting for permission from a record company.

Admittedly, there are costs. Screen addiction is real, online predators exist, and misinformation confuses many people. Nevertheless, these harms can be managed through education and good laws, while the benefits cannot be replaced by anything else.

In conclusion, technology has not made our generation lazy. It has made us connected, informed and ambitious, and that is worth protecting.
