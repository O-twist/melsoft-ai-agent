Is Technology Helping Us?

Technology has changed the life of young South Africans in many ways. In this essay I will discuss the good and the harm and then give my opinion.

On the good side, technology makes learning easier. There are many websites where you can get past exam papers and memos. Learners can also join online study groups, which is useful when you live far from your classmates. Technology also helps young people to start small businesses, for example selling sneakers or doing hair and advertising on social media.

On the harm side, technology can be a distraction. Many learners are on TikTok during class and their marks go down. There is also the danger of scams and strangers who pretend to be someone else online. Lastly, not everyone can afford a smartphone or data, so it can make inequality worse.

In my opinion, technology has done more good than harm, because the good things help your whole future while most of the harm can be avoided if you are careful. Schools should have lessons about online safety so that all learners can benefit.
