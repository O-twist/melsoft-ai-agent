Technology has done more good than harm. I agree with this.

Technology is everywhere. We have phones and laptops and TVs. People use them for many things. I use my phone for games and for school. It is good because it is fast. Before technology people had to write letters and wait long. Now it is quick.

Technology can be bad because people get addicted. My cousin plays games the whole day. But I still think it is good.

So technology is good for young South Africans.
