My Favourite Soccer Match

The best day of my life was when Kaizer Chiefs played Orlando Pirates in the Soweto derby and my father took me to the stadium. The crowd was very loud and everybody was singing. We bought pap and wors outside the gate.

In the first half Pirates scored and my father was very angry. But in the second half Chiefs scored two goals and we were jumping up and down. The vuvuzelas were so loud that my ears were ringing the next day.

After the match we took a taxi home and I fell asleep. I will never forget that day because it was the first time I went to a big stadium. One day I want to play for Chiefs and make my father proud.
