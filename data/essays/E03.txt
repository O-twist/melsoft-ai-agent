More Good Than Harm?

Many adults say that phones are ruining the youth. I partly agree, but I believe that overall technology has helped young South Africans more than it has hurt them.

Firstly, technology helps with education. Apps and websites give us notes, quizzes and videos for free. In my school we do not have enough textbooks, so my friends and I share PDF copies on our phones. Without that, some of us would fail.

Secondly, technology helps us find opportunities. Bursaries, learnerships and university applications are all online now. A person who knows how to search can find help that their parents never knew existed.

However, there is harm as well. Data is expensive, so poor learners are left behind. Social media also makes people compare themselves to others, and this causes depression and low self-esteem. Some learners even get addicted to games.

Even so, I think the good is bigger. The problems can be reduced if schools teach us to use technology wisely and if data becomes cheaper. Technology is a tool, and it depends on the person holding it.
